DataTug TUI Design Language

A practical visual language for DataTug’s terminal interface: dense and data-first, but calm enough for long sessions. The terminal is a canvas; background changes define regions, borders define structure, and bright colour is reserved for state and focus.

Principles

Surfaces before boxesSide panels are borderless and slightly lighter than the main canvas.
Borders mean structureRegions are separated by space and background. Internal separators are used sparingly.
Colour means stateBlue = focus/selection, yellow = warning/attention, green = ready/success.
Dense, not crampedUse one-cell gaps and padding. Tables may be dense; controls need breathing room.

Border rules

No outer boxes around side panels or the status bar. Side panels are separated by their surface colour. The central workspace has no side borders. Gaps and background changes separate regions. Tabs and composer use horizontal separators. Strong/blue borders are reserved for focused controls or structured content, not permanent panel decoration.
